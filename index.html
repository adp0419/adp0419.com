The Gap

An earnings-reaction strategy built on the gap between Polymarket's implied probability of a company beating earnings and that company's own trailing 8-quarter beat rate. A large disagreement between the two predicts how the stock moves the day after the report — evidence held up across 14,477 historical earnings events and 671 real Polymarket-matched contracts.

Backtested Oct 2025–Aug 2026 across 66 S&P 500 companies (94 trades, 49 long / 45 short) with asymmetric long/short thresholds, achieving a 2.08 Sharpe ratio and 8.67% return with a −2.00% max drawdown, validated against a 5,000-path Monte Carlo simulation.

Agents Among Us

Research with UF Data Studio asking whether LLM agents can detect deception in multi-turn dialogue. We built a live social deduction game where agents move, discuss, and vote, logging every decision across 10,134 games and 289,000+ utterances from 19 open-weight models.

Scaled LLM inference by integrating 20+ transformer models (7B–80B parameters) with 4-bit quantization and SLURM multi-GPU scheduling on HiPerGator HPC.

Built the observer pipeline — a scikit-learn NLP classifier (TF-IDF, NLTK preprocessing; Logistic Regression, SVM, MLP) reaching 85–90% F1 on deceptive-agent detection — whose suspicion scores are fed back into the live game.

Built the interactive dashboard with Flask and JavaScript for real-time suspicion scoring and agent visualization, with sub-500ms state refresh across 20+ LLM configurations.

House Pricing Multi Linear Regression

A machine learning model that predicts how much a house will sell for based on features like size, location, number of bedrooms/bathrooms, and condition. Cleaned and prepared a real dataset of ~4,000 U.S. home sales, engineered new features, trained a linear regression model, then used SHAP to explain which features matter most.

Found that Seattle location and above-ground living ratio are the top 2 price drivers, outranking raw square footage across all 132 features. Achieved R²=0.58 and MAE of ~$117K.

Senti

A command-line tool that explains why stocks are going up or down. You type in stock tickers, and it pulls recent news articles, runs them through FinBERT (a transformer trained on financial text) to score sentiment, then compares that sentiment to the stock's actual price movement over the past week.

Uses exponential decay weighting (e^(-0.2t)) so today's news carries full weight and a 7-day-old article counts for about 25%. Outputs a plain-English interpretation — like "news-driven rally" or "technical rebound" — for each ticker.

Climbing the Language Tree

Accepted into the Young Scholars Program at Florida State University. My team and I conducted a cross-linguistic statistical analysis of speech pause behavior under Dr. Michael Kaschak. We analyzed 1,000+ audio recordings across 5 languages (English, Spanish, French, Greek, Hindi).

We found that linguistically related languages share similar pause duration patterns. This was validated with regression models achieving R²=0.72 and p<0.01.
