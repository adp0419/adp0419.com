Sharpe Strategy

A volatility-adjusted momentum trading strategy achieving a 0.9 annual Sharpe ratio and 17% portfolio growth across 25+ liquid assets using inverse volatility weighting and 63-day ranking windows.

Backtested over 10 years of varied market conditions with realistic execution constraints — 5% stop-losses, monthly rebalancing, and 90/10 capital allocation — across equities, ETFs, commodities, and currencies.

Climbing the Language Tree

A cross-linguistic statistical analysis of speech pause behavior conducted at Florida State University. Discovered that linguistically related languages share similar pause duration patterns by analyzing 1,000+ audio recordings across 5 languages.

Validated findings with regression models achieving R²=0.72 and p<0.01, contributing foundational psycholinguistic research with multilingual pause duration datasets and Matplotlib visualizations.

Python R NLTK Matplotlib SciPy
